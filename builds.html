Scatterbrain

Built solo in Lovable for SheBuilds Season 04: everyone chats privately with an AI that quietly nudges their conversations toward shared topics. A live force-directed graph clusters those topics and tracks who's circling which one, but each stays anonymous until another player correctly guesses what it is and claims the label. Scores stay hidden until the round ends, then are revealed together, with the winner's name pinned at the top.

Hollow Tree

Built with Göran Törnquist and Tianlin Shao for the Nebius × NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon: a confidential AI speaking partner, not a therapist, for people who stay silent on stigmatized health topics — puberty, sexual health, men's mental health, questioning their identity. Every answer is grounded in vetted sources like the WHO, with a one-tap quick-exit that flips the screen to a decoy weather app if someone walks in. It's the project that got me hooked on building, not just marketing, AI products — still in progress toward our submission, with voice input and a public link on the way.
