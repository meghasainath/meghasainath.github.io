Health Companion · Lovable Hackathon

A Gender-Responsive Health Chatbot

Built in Lovable, starting from a rough prototype I called Taboo: a confidential chatbot that answers sensitive health questions with grounded, source-cited responses instead of vague reassurance. It's the project that got me hooked on building, not just marketing, AI products.

Multiplayer Game · SheBuilds Hackathon

Scatterbrain

A party game where everyone privately chats with a fact-giving bot, their answers get tagged and clustered in real time, and the group races to guess the emerging consensus as it forms on a shared screen. Built on Supabase for the SheBuilds Season 04 hackathon, and grown out of an earlier prototype, Hollow Tree, where phones became controllers for a shared on-screen game.
