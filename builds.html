Hollow Tree: A Private Space to Ask What You Can't Ask Anyone

Many people carry health questions they feel unable to ask anyone, about their bodies, their identity or their mental health, because of stigma. Hollow Tree is a private AI companion for exactly that moment. It gives confidential, non-clinical guidance grounded in vetted health sources, with no sign-up.

Outcome

A working prototype, still in progress. [Add the hackathon result or any feedback you got, if there is one.]

  • Nothing to give away. No account, name or email. The conversation stays in the browser on the person's own device.
  • Answers with sources. Replies draw on a vetted list of trusted organisations, such as the WHO, UNICEF and the NHS, and show where each answer comes from.
  • A quick exit. One key press, or one tap on a phone, swaps the screen for an ordinary-looking weather page, and the same key brings the conversation back.
  • Clear limits. It acts as a speaking partner, never a therapist. If someone may be in danger, it says it is worried and points to helplines.
